Carnivores

Lion

King of the savanna

Description

Lions are apex predators that primarily hunt large mammals such as zebras and wildebeests. They live in prides and are known for their powerful roars and social hunting strategies.

Porcupine Fish

Ocean oddity

Description

The porcupine fish, also known as the balloonfish, is a fascinating marine creature that can inflate its body with water or air when threatened. Its spiny appearance provides protection from predators in the deep ocean.

Moray Eel

Mysterious reef hunter

Description

Moray eels are fascinating marine predators with elongated bodies and powerful jaws. They inhabit coral reefs and rocky crevices, emerging at night to hunt for fish and crustaceans with their sharp teeth and stealthy movements.

Herbivores

Panda

Bamboo specialist

Description

Giant pandas are primarily herbivorous, with bamboo making up 99% of their diet. These iconic black-and-white bears are native to south central China and are known for their gentle nature and distinctive appearance.

Cow

Gentle grazer

Description

Cows are large herbivorous mammals that primarily eat grass. They have a specialized stomach with four compartments that allows them to efficiently digest plant material through fermentation.

Deer

Forest forager

Description

Deer are ruminant mammals that feed on leaves, grass, and other vegetation. They are known for their antlers and graceful movements through forest and woodland habitats.

Omnivores

Brown Bear

Majestic wilderness

Description

This magnificent brown bear stands as a symbol of wild beauty and strength. Found in forests and mountains, these powerful creatures are omnivores that eat both plants and animals, including berries, fish, and small mammals.

Pig

Intelligent forager

Description

Pigs are highly intelligent omnivores that will eat almost anything, from roots and fruits to insects and small animals. They use their strong snouts to root through soil in search of food.

Raccoon

Masked bandit

Description

Raccoons are clever omnivores known for their dexterous front paws and distinctive black masks. They eat fruits, nuts, insects, eggs, and small animals, often washing their food in water before eating.
